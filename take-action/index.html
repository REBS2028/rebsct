Organizations to support

REBs recommends these organizations for community support.

  • Danbury Unites for Immigrants

    ICE swept through Danbury, CT on the first day of school — abducting parents as soon as their children boarded their bus. Family members left behind struggle to buy basic necessities and need help navigating what comes next.

    Danbury Unites for Immigrants (DUFI) is there to help. Make a donation to help them do their work.

    Learn more and donate Danbury Unites for Immigrants↗

  • Each Step Home

    Since 2025, ICE has detained over 6,200 children with their families in deplorable conditions. Each Step Home supports and protects these children and their families whose mental and physical wellbeing is being threatened by an inhumane immigration system.

    They empower families by providing critical information and resources.

    Support their work and learn more Each Step Home↗
